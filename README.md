# Streakless

**Star trails without the planes.** Free from [James Jones Photography](https://jamesjones.photography).

Star trail stacking that finds and removes aeroplane and satellite streaks automatically, keeps your meteors, and saves a full-quality 32-bit TIFF for Lightroom. It reads your camera's RAW files directly (Canon CR2/CR3, Nikon, Sony, Fuji, DNG and more), so there's no converting first.

## 1 · Download and install

### Download

Go to **[the latest release](../../releases/latest)** and download the file for your computer:

| Computer | File |
|---|---|
| Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) | `Streakless-mac-apple-silicon.zip` |
| Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) | `Streakless-windows.zip` |

### Opening it the first time

Streakless is free and isn't signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft certificate, so your computer asks you to confirm the first time you open it. This is normal for free apps from independent makers.

**Mac**

1. Double-click the zip to unzip it, then drag **Streakless.app** into your **Applications** folder.
2. Double-click it. macOS says it can't verify the app: click **Done**.
3. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down to **Security**, and click **Open Anyway** next to the message about Streakless. Enter your password if asked, then click **Open**.

You only do this once (and again after installing an update).

**Windows**

1. Right-click the zip and choose **Extract All…** (don't run it from inside the zip).
2. Open the **Streakless** folder and double-click **Streakless.exe**.
3. If a blue "Windows protected your PC" box appears, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## 2 · Using Streakless

1. **Choose folder of frames…** and pick the folder with your night's frames (RAW, TIFF or JPG).
2. Press **Find streaks**.
3. On **Review streaks**, check the cards: red ones (planes and satellites) will be removed, green ones (possible meteors) are kept.
4. Press **Stack & save**. Your TIFF and JPG are saved in a `streakless` folder next to your frames. Your original photos are never changed.

**Full step-by-step guide:** [How to use Streakless](GUIDE.md), with screenshots of every step, shooting tips and answers to common problems. It's also in the app under **Help → How to use Streakless**.

## Features

- **Planes and satellites removed automatically.** Every streak is found and shown on its own card, even when it crosses many frames, so you can check each one before stacking.
- **Meteors kept.** Short single-frame streaks are treated as possible meteors and left in, unless you choose to remove them.
- **RAW straight from the camera.** Canon CR2/CR3, Nikon, Sony, Fuji, DNG and more, with no converting first.
- **A full-quality 32-bit TIFF** with a matching colour profile, so it edits like a RAW in Lightroom.
- **Odd frames spotted for you:** headlights, a torch, the lens cap left on or passing cloud are left out, and you can put them back.
- **Hot pixels removed**, with optional dark frames.
- **Gap filling** joins the tiny breaks between frames, so trails look continuous.
- **Comet trails** that fade like a comet's tail, with adjustable strength.
- **Timelapse videos** of the trails growing or of comet trails, in 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5 or the original shape, ready for Instagram, Reels or YouTube.
- **Before/after comparison** that highlights exactly what was removed.
- **Mac and Windows**, free, with update notifications built in.
